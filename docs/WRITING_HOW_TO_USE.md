# Writing Mode — How to Use

A complete guide to Writing Mode in Ministry Hub: a distraction-free, Word-style writing environment for sermons, scripts, journals, or any long-form document — with folders, writing sprints, ambient soundscapes, and scripture lookups built in.

## 1. Getting to Writing Mode

Click **Writing** in the nav rail. You'll land on the **Library** — the home base for every document and folder you've created.

📸 *[Screenshot: Writing Mode topbar with the Ministry Hub logo and "Writing Mode" tab indicator]*

## 2. The Library

The Library has two tabs:

- **Folders** (default) — shows your document folders ("series") as stacked cards.
- **Other Documents** — shows any document that isn't filed into a folder.

📸 *[Screenshot: Library view, Folders tab, with a couple of folder cards and the tab switcher]*

**Along the top of the Library:**
- **New document** — creates a blank document and opens it immediately in the editor.
- **📚 New Folder** — opens a naming modal to create a new folder/series (see [section 3](#3-folders-series)).
- **Resume Last** — reopens whichever document you edited most recently, straight into edit mode. Only appears once you have at least one document.
- **Last session summary** — a small dismissible line like "Last session — 240 words in 12 min," summarizing your most recent writing session.
- **Library stats** — total documents and total words across everything you've written.

If you have no documents yet, the Folders and Other Documents tabs will simply be empty — start with **New document** or **New Folder**.

---

## 3. Folders (Series)

Folders group related documents together — useful for a sermon series, a devotional set, or chapters of the same project.

📸 *[Screenshot: a folder card showing name, description, document count, and hover-revealed rename/delete icons]*

**Creating a folder:**
1. Click **📚 New Folder**.
2. Type a **name** (required).
3. Optionally add a **description** — this shows on the folder's card face in the Library.
4. Press **Enter** or click **Create**. Press **Esc** to cancel.

**On the folder card:**
- Folder icon, name, and document count ("3 docs").
- The description you set, or a placeholder ("No description yet — open to add documents") if you left it blank.
- Hover to reveal **✎ Rename** and **🗑 Delete** buttons.
- Click anywhere else on the card to drill in.

**Inside a folder (Series view):**
- **← All documents** takes you back to the Library.
- Header shows the folder's name, description, document count, and total word count.
- **+ New document in this folder** creates a document already filed into this series.
- All documents in the series are listed below; if there are none yet, you'll see an empty state.

**Deleting a folder** asks for confirmation first. Documents inside it are **not** deleted — they become un-filed and move to the Other Documents tab.

## 4. Creating & Opening Documents

**New document** — from the Library or from inside a folder — creates a blank, untitled document (Letter size, portrait, normal margins) and drops you straight into the Focus Editor to start typing.

**Opening an existing document** — click its card in the Library, Series view, or Other Documents tab. Documents opened from a **writing nudge** (bell icon) open in **view mode** instead of edit mode — see [section 14](#14-view-only-mode).

**Moving a document to a different folder** — use the context menu on the document's card.

**Empty documents are auto-discarded.** If you open a new document, type nothing, and exit, it's deleted rather than saved as an orphaned "Untitled" entry — so don't worry about cluttering your library by peeking into the editor and backing out.

## 5. The Focus Editor

Opening a document takes over the full screen with a simulated paper page on a soft gray "desk" background — designed to keep you focused on the words, not the chrome.

📸 *[Screenshot: Focus Editor with a page of text, ribbon visible at top, footer status bar at bottom]*

**Top bar:**
- **← Exit** — saves and returns to the Library. (Also triggered by `Esc`.)
- **Title** — click into it and type; saves automatically a moment after you stop typing.
- **Word goal bar** — appears once you've set a goal for this document; fills and turns green as you approach/hit it.
- Flow toggles, sprint control, soundscape control, theme dots, and font size buttons live on the right — see [section 8](#8-flow-tools) onward.

**Auto-hiding chrome:** the ribbon and top bar fade out after a few seconds of inactivity so nothing but your page is on screen. Move your mouse or start typing and it reappears instantly.

**Footer status bar:** live word count, page count, estimated reading time, save status ("Saving…" / "Saved"), and zoom controls.

**Themes:** four color dots let you switch the page's look — **Paper** (off-white), **Sepia** (warm tan), **Midnight** (dark), and **Forest** (dark green). This preference is remembered across all your documents.

**Font size:** **A−** / **A+** buttons shrink or grow the page's base font size; also remembered globally.

## 6. The Ribbon (Formatting Tools)

The ribbon mimics a familiar word processor, organized into tabs:

📸 *[Screenshot: ribbon showing the Home tab's font, paragraph, and styles groups]*

**Home** — undo/redo; font family and size; bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, sub/superscript; sentence/lower/UPPER/Title case; clear formatting; font color and highlight color; bullet, numbered, and checklist lists; indent/outdent; formatting marks (¶) toggle; text alignment; line spacing; block styles (Normal, Title, Heading 1, Heading 2, Quote); Find and Find & Replace.

**Insert** — insert a table; insert a picture from your computer (with alignment tools and a drag-to-resize handle once placed); a symbol palette (em dashes, arrows, math symbols, and more); add a header or footer band; toggle page numbers; insert today's date; insert a horizontal line; insert a **scripture verse** (see [section 7](#7-scripture-lookup)); force a page break.

**Layout** — page margins (Normal, Narrow, Moderate, Wide), orientation (Portrait/Landscape), and page size (Letter, A4, Legal).

**References** — insert or update a **Table of Contents**, automatically built from your Heading 1/2 and Title styles.

**View** — toggle the Outline pane and formatting marks; zoom controls; word count popover; Print; Export to Word (.doc); and a **Focus** button that hides all chrome for maximum distraction-free writing.

**Keyboard shortcuts:** `Ctrl/Cmd+B` bold, `Ctrl/Cmd+I` italic, `Ctrl/Cmd+U` underline, `Ctrl/Cmd+F` find, `Ctrl/Cmd+H` find & replace, `Ctrl/Cmd+S` save now, `Esc` exit (or close an open popover first).

## 7. Scripture Lookup

From the **Insert** tab, use the **Verse/Scripture** tool to drop a Bible passage straight into your document:

📸 *[Screenshot: scripture insert popover with a reference field, translation picker, and inserted verse preview]*

1. Type a reference, e.g. `John 3:16` or `Psalm 23`.
2. Pick a translation — **KJV, WEB, BBE, OEB,** or **ASV**.
3. The verse is fetched automatically and inserted as a formatted, citation-labeled quote block.

If the reference can't be found (typo, or no internet connection), you'll see an error instead of a silent failure — double-check the reference and try again.

## 8. Flow Tools

These sit in the top bar in edit mode and are meant to help you stay immersed:

📸 *[Screenshot: flow toggle buttons in the top bar — grammar check, dimming, typewriter, hardcore]*

- **✅ Grammar Check** — opens a side panel that flags spelling slips, doubled words, spacing/punctuation issues, and common grammar mix-ups (e.g. "could of" → "could have"). It's a built-in pattern checker, not an external service — nothing you write is sent anywhere for this. Click **🔄** in the panel to re-check after edits.
- **◐ Paragraph Dimming** — fades every paragraph except the one your cursor is in, so your eye stays on the current thought.
- **⌖ Typewriter Scrolling** — keeps your current line vertically centered on screen as you type, like an old typewriter carriage.
- **🔒 Hardcore Mode** — disables backspace/delete entirely, forcing a forward-only rough draft. The page gets an amber border and the footer shows "🔒 no deleting" as a reminder while it's on.

All four preferences are remembered across documents.

## 9. Writing Sprints

Click the **⏱ Sprint** button to start a timed writing session.

📸 *[Screenshot: sprint dropdown showing 5/10/15/25-minute presets and a live countdown]*

1. Pick a preset — **5, 10, 15,** or **25 minutes**.
2. The button switches to a live countdown ("12:34 · Stop") while the sprint runs.
3. When time's up, a chime plays and a toast appears showing how many words you wrote during the sprint — dismiss it with **Keep writing** to carry on.

You can stop a sprint early by clicking **Stop**.

## 10. Soundscapes

Click the **🎧** button to open ambient background sound options — generated live in your browser, no audio files involved.

📸 *[Screenshot: soundscape menu with Rain, Ocean, Wind, Deep noise, White noise options and a volume slider]*

**Options:** Off, **Rain**, **Ocean waves**, **Wind**, **Deep noise** (brown noise), **White noise**.

- Pick any non-off option to start it; the first time you do, your browser may need a moment to initialize audio (this is normal — browsers require a click before they'll play sound).
- Use the **volume slider** to adjust intensity.
- Your last-picked soundscape and volume are remembered for next time.
- Sound stops automatically when you exit the Focus Editor.

## 11. Word Goals & Word Count

Set a target word count for a document using the **word goal** field near the top bar. Once set, a progress bar appears showing your current count against the goal, turning green when you hit it.

For more detail, click **📊 Count** on the View tab to see a popover with word count, character count (with and without spaces), and an estimated reading time.

## 12. Find & Replace, Outline, Grammar Check

- **Find & Replace** (`Ctrl/Cmd+F` / `Ctrl/Cmd+H`) opens a small bar at the top of the page: search, replace-with, **Find**, **Replace** (current match), **All** (replace every match), and a close button.
- **Outline** (View tab) opens a left-hand pane listing every heading in your document, indented by level — click any heading to jump straight to it.
- **Grammar Check** (see [section 8](#8-flow-tools)) opens a right-hand pane of flagged issues with suggested fixes.

📸 *[Screenshot: Find & Replace bar open at the top of the page, Outline pane on the left]*

## 13. Page Setup, Print & Export

Under the **Layout** tab, choose your page **size** (Letter, A4, Legal), **orientation** (Portrait/Landscape), and **margins** (Normal, Narrow, Moderate, Wide). The page re-paginates automatically as you type or adjust these settings, adding blank pages as content grows.

From the **View** tab:
- **🖨 Print** sends the document to your printer.
- **⬇ Word** exports the document as a Word-compatible `.doc` file you can open in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

📸 *[Screenshot: Layout tab open, showing margin/orientation/size dropdowns]*

## 14. View-Only Mode

Documents opened from a **writing nudge** (bell icon) open in **view mode**: the page is read-only, the ribbon is hidden, and a **👁 Viewing** badge appears in the top bar. Grammar Check is still available. Click **✎ Edit** any time to switch into full edit mode.

📸 *[Screenshot: top bar in view mode showing the "Viewing" badge and Edit button]*

## 15. Saving, Autosave & Multi-Device Sync

Writing Mode autosaves as you type — there's no manual save button you need to remember, though `Ctrl/Cmd+S` will force an immediate save if you want the peace of mind. The footer shows **"Saving…"** briefly, then **"Saved."**

Each document is stored as its own record (rather than everything living in one big blob), which keeps large documents — especially ones with embedded images — from hitting storage size limits, and means one big document saving slowly won't hold up any of your others. Changes sync across devices automatically once saved; opening the same document elsewhere shows the latest saved version.

## 16. Writing Nudges

Click the **🔔 bell** in the topbar to see writing nudges:

📸 *[Screenshot: bell popover showing a "haven't written today" nudge and an unmet-goal nudge]*

- **"You haven't written today"** — appears if you haven't touched any document yet today; click it to jump into your most recent document.
- **Unmet word goal nudges** — one per document that has a goal set but hasn't reached it, e.g. "250 words to your goal."

Clicking a nudge opens that document in **view mode** (see [section 14](#14-view-only-mode)).

## 17. Troubleshooting

| Situation | What's happening |
|---|---|
| Soundscape doesn't play when first selected | Browsers block audio until you've clicked something on the page — click the soundscape option again, or click anywhere on the page first. |
| Scripture verse won't insert | The reference may be misspelled, or you're offline — check the reference format (e.g. `John 3:16`) and your connection. |
| Backspace/Delete does nothing | **Hardcore Mode** is on (🔒 in the flow toggles) — turn it off to edit freely again. |
| A blank "Untitled" document I opened by accident disappeared | This is expected — empty documents (no title, body, or images) are discarded automatically when you exit. |
| Document doesn't show my latest edits on another device | Give autosave a moment to finish ("Saving…" → "Saved") on the original device before switching. |
