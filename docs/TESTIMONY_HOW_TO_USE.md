## 3. Locked Fields — Why Text Boxes Start Read-Only

Every text field on this page — story sections, key verse, condensed versions, practice notes, 3 Circles phrasing — starts **locked** (read-only, shown with a "Double-click to edit" tooltip).

- **Double-click** (or double-tap) a field to unlock it and start typing.
- The field **re-locks automatically** when you click away (on blur).

This is intentional: these fields are meant to be reflected on, not dashed off, so an accidental click while you're reading or praying won't disturb what's already written.

## 4. My Story

The **📖 My Story** tab is where you write your salvation testimony in three parts, add a key verse, and boil it down into shorter versions.

📸 *[Screenshot: My Story tab showing the progress bar and the three story cards]*

**Progress card** — a bar at the top showing what percentage of the three core sections you've filled in, with a reminder that each should be short, honest, and specific.

**Three story sections:**

| Section | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 🌑 **Before Christ** | What was your life like before you knew Jesus? What were you searching for? What did you trust in? |
| ✨ **How I Met Jesus** | How did you come to know Christ? Who shared the gospel? What moment(s) drew you to repentance and faith? |
| 🌅 **Life with Christ** | What has changed since? How is God still shaping you? What hope do you have today? |

Each card shows a live **word count** under the text box.

**📜 Key Verse** — a scripture that anchors your testimony, meant to be used as a bridge into the story or a closing line.

**Condensed Versions** — below the full story, two shorter drafts to prepare separately:

- **⚡ Elevator (30 seconds)** — three sentences: where you were, what changed, where you are now.
- **☕ Short (2 minutes)** — a coffee-shop-length version that still shares the gospel through your story.

These two condensed drafts feed directly into the **Practice** tab's scripts.

## 5. Practice

The **⏱ Practice** tab is a rehearsal timer for whichever version of your story you want to sharpen.

📸 *[Screenshot: Practice tab with version picker and the running timer]*

**Pick your version** — three buttons, each with a target time and (once you've saved attempts) a running average:

| Version | Target time | Script source |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ Elevator (30s) | 30 seconds | Pulled from your **elevator** draft on My Story |
| ☕ Short (2 min) | 2 minutes | Pulled from your **short** draft on My Story |
| 📖 Full Story | 5 minutes | No linked script — this is your unabridged testimony |

**Running the timer:**
- **▶ Start / ▶ Resume** — begins or continues the clock.
- **⏸ Pause** — stops it without resetting.
- **↺ Reset** — clears the elapsed time back to zero.
- The clock face changes color as you approach and pass your target (on-pace → close → over).
- **📜 Show your [version] script** — an expandable panel showing the matching draft from My Story. If you haven't written that version yet, it prompts you to head to My Story first.
- A notes field lets you jot what flowed or felt clunky before saving.
- **💾 Save attempt** — records the duration, target, and your notes to History (disabled until the timer has run at least once).

**History** — every saved attempt, newest first, showing the duration, how far over/under target it was (±5s tolerance before it's flagged), the version, the date, and your notes. Each entry can be deleted with the 🗑 button.

## 6. 3 Circles

The **⭕ 3 Circles** tab walks you through the 3-Circles evangelism diagram, letting you capture your own phrasing for each part and practice sketching it.

📸 *[Screenshot: 3 Circles tab showing the whiteboard and the diagram nodes]*

**Practice drawing (whiteboard)** — a blank canvas you can draw on directly with mouse or touch, to build muscle memory for sketching the diagram on a napkin or whiteboard in real life. Click **🧹 Erase** to clear it.

**The five steps** — click any node in the diagram to jump to it, or use the **← / →** navigation at the bottom of the detail panel. A dot appears on any node you've already written something for.

| Step | Summary | Key verse |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **God's Design** | Life flourishes in relationship with God — peace, purpose, joy. | Genesis 1:1 |
| 🔴 **Brokenness** | We rejected God's design, causing brokenness in us, others, and the world. | Romans 3:23 |
| ✝️ **Gospel** | Jesus lived the life we couldn't, died the death we deserved, rose to give new life. | 1 Peter 3:18 |
| 🙌 **Repent & Believe** | Turn from sin and trust Jesus to receive the gift. | Mark 1:15 |
| 🌱 **Recover & Pursue** | Restored to God's design, we pursue Him daily. | 2 Corinthians 5:17 |

For each step, write your own explanation in the text box provided — the goal is to have the words ready before the moment you need them.

📸 *[Screenshot: a single 3 Circles step detail panel, e.g. "Gospel," with the text box filled in]*

## 7. More Methods

The **🌉 More Methods** tab is a reference — not something you fill in — covering two other classic gospel-sharing diagrams to keep alongside the 3 Circles.

📸 *[Screenshot: More Methods tab showing both Bridge to Life and Romans Road cards]*

**🌉 Bridge to Life** — a drawing of a chasm bridged by the cross, in four steps: God Loves You (John 3:16) → Our Problem: Sin (Romans 6:23a) → God's Solution (Romans 5:8) → Our Response (Romans 10:9).

**🛣 Romans Road** — a path of verses straight from Romans, in five steps: All have sinned (Romans 3:23) → The wages of sin is death (Romans 6:23) → God demonstrates His love (Romans 5:8) → Confess and believe (Romans 10:9–10) → Peace with God (Romans 5:1).

Each step lists a title, a short explanation, and its verse reference for quick recall.

## 8. Where Your Data Lives

Everything on this page — your story, key verse, condensed versions, 3 Circles phrasing, and practice history — is saved automatically to a single document per user (`/users/{uid}/kv/testimony:hub`). There's no separate save button for the page as a whole; individual actions (editing a field, saving a practice attempt) persist immediately.
