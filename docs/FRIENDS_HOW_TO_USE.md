A complete guide to the Friends page in Ministry Hub: keeping a directory of the people in your life, staying on top of reach-outs and birthdays, and tracking prayers, notes, and events per person.

## 1. Getting to the Friends Page

Click **Friends** in the nav rail. You'll land on the **📇 Directory** view by default; you can switch to **💓 Pulse** any time from the topbar.

## 2. Directory View

A photo-first grid of everyone in your hub, sorted so the people who need attention rise to the top.

📸 *[Screenshot: Directory grid with several friend cards, cadence bar, and filter chips]*

**Cadence Summary Bar** — clickable stat tiles across the top:
- **Overdue + Today** (red) — "X overdue · Y today," or "All caught up" if there's nothing pending. Click to filter the grid to just these people.
- **Upcoming** (orange) — friends due for a reach-out soon.
- **No Schedule** (gray) — friends without any reach-out cadence set.
- **✨ Start Reach-Out** — appears whenever someone is overdue or due today; launches [Reach-Out Mode](#6-reach-out-mode).

**Search & Sort:**
- **Search** — matches name, role, church, or tags in real time.
- **Sort by** — "Reach-out (urgent first)" *(default)*, "Most recently contacted," or "Name (A–Z)."

**Filter chips** — quick one-click filters: All, 🔴 Reach out, 🎂 Birthdays, 💛 Close, 👨‍👩‍👧 Family, ⛪ Church, 🙏 Prayer, 🌳 Mentor, 🌱 Mentee, 💼 Co-worker.

**Each friend card shows:**
- A colored top stripe (neutral / overdue / due-today / birthday)
- A badge in the corner when relevant: ⚡ "Xd late," ⚡ "Today," or 🎂 "Birthday!" / "Xd"
- Avatar (photo, custom avatar, or color-gradient initials), name, nickname, role · church
- Friend status (e.g. 💛 Close friend)
- Last contact date (or "Never")
- A small 📅 indicator if any dates are synced to your Calendar

Click any card to open that person in the [detail panel](#5-the-friend-detail-panel).

**If you have no friends yet**, you'll see a welcome card: "Your hub of people," a short 3-step explainer, and an "✦ Add your first friend" button.

## 3. Pulse View

Switch to **💓 Pulse** for a more visual, "at a glance" read on your relationships.

📸 *[Screenshot: Pulse view in orbit mode, showing avatar orbs arranged in warmth bands]*

- **Orbit mode** (default) — you're at the center, and friends orbit around you in concentric bands: **Warm** (in-rhythm), **Due**, **Cooling**, and **Drifting** — the further out, the longer it's been.
- **Grid mode** — the same people shown as a simple card grid, each color-coded by the same warmth tone (overdue = red, due = orange, warm = green, no schedule = gray).

Both modes are just different lenses on the same underlying reach-out data as the Directory view — clicking any orb or card opens that friend's detail panel.

## 4. Adding a Friend

Click **✦ Add Friend** in the topbar for the full form, or use the **+ Quick Add** floating button for a fast, minimal entry.

📸 *[Screenshot: Add Friend form showing avatar builder and "Who they are" section]*

**Full form sections:**

1. **Avatar** — click to open the avatar builder and customize a face, or a photo will be used if picked from Contacts.
2. **Who they are** — full name (required; typing here can autocomplete from your accepted Contacts and auto-fill church, photo, and tags), nickname, friend status, role, church/organization, city, birthday.
3. **Backstory** — how you met, current life season, and a life update/what they're going through.
4. **Reach-out schedule** — pick specific weekdays with the day picker (plus presets: Daily, Weekdays, Weekends, M/W/F, Clear), or fall back to a simple cadence (Daily / Weekly / Every 2 weeks / Monthly). You can also set their current emotional status.
5. **Tags & Interests** — free-form chips you add by typing and pressing Enter.

Click **✓ Add Friend** to save (disabled until a name is entered).

**Quick Add** just asks for a name and, optionally, a friend status — everything else (birthday, cadence, notes) can be filled in later from their card.

## 5. The Friend Detail Panel

Clicking a friend opens a slide-over panel on the right with four tabs. Press **Esc** or click the ✕/overlay to close it.

📸 *[Screenshot: Friend detail slide-over, Profile tab, showing reach-out and birthday banners]*

**Header banners:**
- A **reach-out status banner** — overdue (red), due today (orange), or upcoming — showing days overdue/until, last contact date, and the schedule.
- A **birthday banner** if their birthday falls within the next 60 days ("Birthday today!", "Birthday tomorrow," or "Birthday in X days").
- **✓ Reached Out Today** button — one click logs today as the last-contact date.
- **🗑 Remove** button — deletes the friend (with confirmation).

### 👤 Profile & Reach Out
View mode shows life season, what they're going through, how you met, interests, friend status, reach-out schedule, birthday, and quick notes. Click **✎ Edit** to change any of these, including the reach-out day picker or cadence, and to toggle **Calendar sync** for 🎂 birthday and ⚡ reach-out schedule individually.

### 🙏 Prayer Requests
Every prayer or praise linked to this person, grouped into Prayer Requests and Praise & Thanksgiving. Click a card to expand it (shows content, scripture, and recent updates) or click **🔗 Open in Prayers tab** to jump to the full prayer. A **"✦ New Prayer for [Name]"** button starts a new prayer pre-linked to them.

📸 *[Screenshot: Prayer Requests tab with an expanded prayer card]*

### 📅 Events & Birthdays
Shows their birthday card (with a synced indicator if enabled) plus any custom events you've added, each with its own **Calendar sync** checkbox. Use the composer at the bottom (title, date, optional description) and click **+ Add Event**.

### 📝 Notes
A running timeline — type in the composer and click **+ Add Note** to log a conversation, encouragement, or anything worth remembering. Entries are shown newest-first with a timestamp.

📸 *[Screenshot: Notes tab with timeline entries]*

## 6. Reach-Out Mode

Click **✨ Reach Out** in the topbar (badge shows how many people are due) to walk through everyone who's overdue or due today, one at a time.

📸 *[Screenshot: Reach-Out Mode person card with urgency banner and medium buttons]*

**For each person**, you'll see their avatar, name, friend status/role/church, an urgency banner (e.g. "3 days overdue" / "Due today," with last-contact date), and any life update or life season notes.

**One-tap logging buttons** — 📞 Called, 💬 Texted, 📧 Emailed, ☕ In person — each logs that contact method, records a timeline note, and automatically advances to the next person.

**Other controls:**
- **✎ Open their card** — opens the full detail panel without leaving Reach-Out mode
- **Skip** — advance without logging anything
- **Done, Next →** / **Finish** — moves on (or wraps up on the last person)

When you finish, you'll see a completion screen ("You connected with X person/people") with a Galatians 6:2 quote, and the option to walk through again or head back to the Directory.

If nobody is due, you'll see "You're all caught up" instead.

## 7. Notifications

The **🔔 activity bell** in the topbar shows a combined count of overdue reach-outs and today's birthdays.

📸 *[Screenshot: activity bell popover with a reach-out item and a birthday item]*

- **Reach-out items** — show days overdue (or "due today") with a one-click **✓ Mark reached out** button.
- **Birthday items** — show "🎂 Birthday today."
- Each item can be dismissed individually (✕); dismissals last for the session and clear automatically once the underlying status changes (e.g. you reach out to them) or the page reloads.
- A **"Start Reach Out →"** button in the popover jumps straight into [Reach-Out Mode](#6-reach-out-mode).

The same overdue + birthday count also shows as a badge on the Friends icon in the main nav rail.

## 8. Calendar Sync

Three independent things can sync to your Calendar page, all opt-in per friend:

- **🎂 Birthday** — toggle in the Profile tab (disabled until a birthday is set).
- **⚡ Reach-out schedule** — toggle in the Profile tab; creates recurring entries matching their reach-out days/cadence.
- **Individual events** — each custom event added in the Events tab has its own sync checkbox.

📸 *[Screenshot: Profile tab edit mode showing the two Calendar sync checkboxes]*

## 9. Integrations with Prayers & Contacts

- **Prayers** — linking is bidirectional. Add a friend to a prayer from either the Prayers page or the friend's **🙏 Prayer Requests** tab, and it shows up in both places.
- **Contacts** — when adding a friend, typing a name that matches an accepted Contact connection lets you auto-fill their church, photo, and tags instead of typing everything by hand.

## 10. Troubleshooting & Error Messages

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Remove this friend?" | Confirmation shown before permanently deleting a friend and their history. |
| Add Friend button stays disabled | The name field is empty — a name is required to save. |
| Add Event button stays disabled | Both a title and a date are required for a friend event. |
| "Filling from their profile..." | The form is pulling in details after you picked a matching Contact — wait a moment for fields to populate. |
| "No prayers linked to this friend yet." | This person hasn't been linked to any prayer requests — add one from the Prayers page or the "New Prayer for [Name]" button. |
| "No notes yet." | The Notes tab timeline is empty — log your first note in the composer. |
