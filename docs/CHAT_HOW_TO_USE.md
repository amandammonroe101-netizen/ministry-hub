# Chat — How to Use

A complete guide to the Chat page in Ministry Hub: finding and creating channels, sending messages and files, managing members, and picking a channel theme.

> 🛠 **Something not working?** Go to **Settings → Feedback & Requests** and click **+ New Request** to submit a bug report — it goes straight to the Founder.

## 1. Getting to the Chat Page

Click **Chat** in the nav rail. You'll land on the channel list; open any joined channel to start messaging.

---

## 2. Public vs. Private Channels

Every channel is one of two types. **🌐 Public** channels show up in search for anyone signed in, and can be joined with a single click. **🔒 Private** channels are invite-only — they never appear in search unless you've been invited.

---

## 3. Finding & Joining Channels

📸 *[Screenshot: Channel search bar with Public/Private filter chips]*

- Use **Search channels by name or description…** at the top to find public channels — matches happen as you type.
- When the search bar is empty, the list shows only channels you've already joined. Use the **🌐 Public** / **🔒 Private** filter chips to narrow it down.
- A public channel you haven't joined shows a **+ Join** button on its card — click it to join and open the channel.

---

## 4. Creating a Channel

Click **+ New channel** and fill in a name (required), an optional description, and pick 🌐 Public or 🔒 Private. You can invite people right away by searching your Contacts or pasting their identifying key — each becomes a removable chip. Invited people get an invitation card waiting for them in their own 📨 Requests tab.

📸 *[Screenshot: New channel dialog with name, description, type, and invite fields]*

---

## 5. Requests & Invitations

- **Invitations to you** — shows the channel name, description, and who invited you; **✓ Accept** to join, **✕ Decline** to reject.
- **Invitations you've sent** — shows a **Cancel invite** button for anything still awaiting a response.
- Inside an open channel, click **+ Invite** in the header to invite more people at any time, the same way.
- The Requests chip shows a badge with your pending count.

---

## 6. Sending Messages

📸 *[Screenshot: Conversation view with compose box, emoji picker, and attachment preview]*

- Type in the box at the bottom and click **Send** (pressing Return does not send).
- 🙂 opens the emoji picker — browse by category or search, then click to insert.
- 📎 attaches a file of any kind — images show an inline preview before you send; other files show a filename + size card. Add an optional caption, or click ✕ to discard before sending.
- A message that's *only* emoji renders extra-large with no bubble.
- Links in a message are automatically made clickable.
- Double-click any attachment in the thread to open it full-screen.

---

## 7. Deleting Messages

Hover a message to reveal its 🗑 icon — you can always delete your own messages, and the channel owner can delete anyone's. Deleting asks for confirmation first and removes it for everyone. There's no message editing — delete and resend instead.

---

## 8. Channel Settings

Open a channel and click the ⚙ gear in its header for:

📸 *[Screenshot: Channel Settings dialog — Members, Chat theme, Files & photos]*

- **Members** — see everyone in the channel; the owner is badged "Owner." The owner can remove members or hand ownership to someone else with **Give Ownership**.
- **Chat theme** — owner-only. Pick from a set of preset color themes (Default, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Lavender, Rose, Amber, Slate, and more) — it changes the channel's background and accent colors for every member.
- **Files & photos** — every attachment ever shared in the channel, newest first; click one to preview it, or delete it if you're the uploader or the owner.
- At the bottom: owners see **🗑 Delete Chat** (removes the channel for everyone); everyone else sees **🚪 Remove from Chat** (leaves the channel). Owners must hand off ownership before they can leave instead of delete.

---

## 9. Unread Badges

Channel cards show a badge with your unread message count; it clears the moment you open that channel. The 📨 Requests chip badges your pending invitation count separately.

---

## 10. Integrations

- **Contacts** — the 💬 Chat button on an accepted contact starts a private 1:1 channel with them here; it stays hidden on their side until they accept.
- Only your accepted Contacts and people you've previously invited show up when searching for someone to invite — Chat doesn't search the full member directory.

---

## 11. Troubleshooting

- **Send** stays disabled until you've typed something or attached a file.
- **"You no longer have access to that chat."** — you were removed from (or, for a public channel, banned from) the channel; it will no longer appear in your list.
- A rules-related error when joining or inviting usually means the app's security rules need to be re-published — that's a founder-side fix, so report it via Settings → Feedback & Requests.
- No typing indicators, read receipts, message editing, threads/replies, or reactions yet — messaging is intentionally kept simple for now.
