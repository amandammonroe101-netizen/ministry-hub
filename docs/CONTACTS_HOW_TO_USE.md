# Contacts — How to Use

A complete guide to the Contacts page in Ministry Hub: finding fellow members, sending and managing connection requests, and starting a chat once you're connected.

> 🛠 **Something not working?** Go to **Settings → Feedback & Requests** and click **+ New Request** to submit a bug report — it goes straight to the Founder.

> **A note on the pictures in this guide:** the Contacts page sits behind Firebase sign-in, so this document couldn't be auto-illustrated with live screenshots of your account. ## 1. Getting to the Contacts Page

Click **Contacts** in the nav rail. The page opens on the **🔍 Search** tab by default.

📸 *[Screenshot: Contacts topbar with the Search / Requests / My Contacts tabs]*

---

## 2. Search

The default tab. Type a name, email, or someone's exact **connect key** into the search bar to find them — results load as you type. Your own connect key is shown just below the search bar; share it with someone so they can find you directly.

📸 *[Screenshot: Search tab with results grid]*

- Each result card shows an avatar, name, and email (if they've made it visible).
- Click a card to open that person's read-only profile — name, ministry name, ministry type(s), bio, and connect key, each with a copy button.
- The button on a card changes based on your relationship with them: **+ Connect**, **Cancel request** (already sent), **Accept request** (they invited you), or **✓ Connected**.

---

## 3. Requests

Shows two lists: **Incoming** requests from people who want to connect with you, and **Sent** requests you're still waiting on.

📸 *[Screenshot: Requests tab with Incoming and Sent sections]*

- Incoming — click **Accept** to connect, or **Decline** to dismiss.
- Sent — shows a **Pending** pill; click **Cancel** to withdraw the request.
- A badge with the incoming count appears on the Requests tab whenever you have unanswered requests.

---

## 4. My Contacts

Everyone whose connection request has been accepted, on either side.

📸 *[Screenshot: My Contacts tab with a connected contact row]*

- Click a name to view their profile.
- **💬 Chat** — starts a private 1:1 chat with that contact on the Chat page (or lets you invite them into a public channel you already belong to). It reuses Chat's own invitation flow, so the invite shows up on their Chat page exactly like any other channel invite.
- **Remove** — ends the connection. It disappears from both people's My Contacts.
- A badge appears on the My Contacts tab when a new connection has been made that you haven't seen yet; opening the tab clears it (also clears the matching badge on the nav rail).

---

## 5. Integrations

- **Chat** — the 💬 Chat button on a contact starts or reuses a private channel; it stays hidden on their side until they accept the invite.
- **Friends** — typing a name on the Friends page that matches an accepted Contacts connection auto-fills their church, photo, and tags.

---

## 6. Troubleshooting

- **No matches for "…"** — try a different name, email, or their exact connect key (partial keys won't match).
- **No contacts yet** — accepted connection requests will show up on this tab; send a request from Search first.
- Buttons show **…** or **Sending…** while a request is in flight — wait for it to finish before retrying.
