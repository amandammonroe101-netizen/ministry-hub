# Calendar — How to Use

A complete guide to the Calendar page in Ministry Hub: navigating the grid, adding your own events, filtering what's shown, and understanding what syncs in automatically from other pages.

## 1. Getting to the Calendar Page

Click **Calendar** in the nav rail. You'll land on Month view by default.

📸 *[Screenshot: Calendar topbar with Today / ‹ / › and the New Event button]*

---

## 2. Navigating the Grid

- Switch between **Month** and **Week** view in the left sidebar under "View."
- **Today**, **‹**, and **›** in the topbar move you around; the header shows the month (or week) you're looking at.
- Click a day to select it — its details open in the right sidebar. Double-click a day to add a new event on it.

---

## 3. Adding an Event

Click **✦ New Event** in the topbar, double-click a day, or click **+ Add event on this day** in the right sidebar once a day is selected. Fill in a title and date (both required), and optionally a description, time, and one of 8 preset colors, then **✓ Save Event**.

📸 *[Screenshot: New Event form]*

---

## 4. Editing & Deleting

Events you created yourself (marked ⭐) show a 🗑 delete button in the right sidebar — deleting asks for confirmation first. There's no inline editing; delete and re-add if something needs to change. Events synced in from another page (see below) can't be edited or deleted here at all — go to the page that created them instead.

---

## 5. Color-Coding & Filters

📸 *[Screenshot: Left sidebar filter groups]*

Every event type has its own emoji and color, grouped by ministry in the left sidebar (Youth, Children's, Worship, Web, Journal, Prayers, Day Planner, Friends, and your own Other/custom events). Each group has a master checkbox — click it to toggle every item in that group on or off at once — or expand the group to flip individual types on/off. Most filters start off so your calendar opens clean; Prayer reminders and "Day Is Planned" start on.

---

## 6. What Syncs In Automatically

A lot of what shows up here is pushed in from other pages rather than added by hand. These are read-only — click the **🔗 Open** button on an event's card to jump to the page that owns it:

- **Friends** — birthdays, reach-out due dates, and custom friend events, each opt-in per friend (toggled on the Friends page).
- **Prayers** — prayer reminders you've opted into via Prayers → Page Settings → Reminders → "Sync to Calendar."
- **Journal** — journal reminders and memory-verse review dates, opted in the same way from Journal → Page Settings → Reminders.
- **Worship, Youth, Children's, and Web ministry pages** — meetings, services, recording sessions, review/renewal due dates, and blog dates sync automatically from their source pages.
- **Day Planner** — instead of one chip per time block, a synced day shows a single "✓ Day Is Planned" pill; click its **📋 Day Planner** button to open that day.

---

## 7. Reminders & Notifications

The bell in the topbar combines due reminders with any pending recording-session requests sent to you — accept a request to import the session and add it to your calendar, or dismiss it to ignore.

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

- **✓ Save Event** stays disabled until both Title and Date are filled in.
- Don't see a ministry's filter group? It's hidden because that category is turned off in Settings → Visible Categories — turn it on there first.
- An event you expected to sync isn't showing? Double-check its sync toggle on the source page (e.g. a friend's "sync_birthday" or "sync_reach_out"), then confirm the matching filter is checked here.
- There's no search box on this page — use the filters and Month/Week navigation to find what you're looking for.
